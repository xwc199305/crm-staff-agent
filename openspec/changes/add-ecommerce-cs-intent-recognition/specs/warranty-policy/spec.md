## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: System can provide warranty policy information
The system SHALL provide accurate warranty policy information to users, including coverage, duration, and exclusions.

#### Scenario: Answer warranty duration questions
- **WHEN** user asks about warranty duration
- **THEN** system SHALL provide the standard warranty period
- **AND** mention any extended warranty options

#### Scenario: Explain warranty coverage
- **WHEN** user asks what is covered under warranty
- **THEN** system SHALL list covered items and services
- **AND** clearly state exclusions and limitations

#### Scenario: Guide user through warranty claim process
- **WHEN** user wants to file a warranty claim
- **THEN** system SHALL provide step-by-step instructions
- **AND** list required documents and contact information

#### Scenario: Provide warranty registration information
- **WHEN** user asks about warranty registration
- **THEN** system SHALL explain the registration process
- **AND** mention benefits of registering

### Requirement: Warranty policy handler follows company standards
The system SHALL provide warranty information that is consistent with company policies and legal requirements.

#### Scenario: Consistent policy information
- **WHEN** multiple users ask the same warranty question
- **THEN** system SHALL provide consistent answers
- **AND** reference the official policy document

#### Scenario: Legal compliance
- **WHEN** providing warranty information
- **THEN** system SHALL comply with applicable laws and regulations
- **AND** include appropriate disclaimers
